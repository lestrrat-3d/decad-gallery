A straight-toothed involute gear: a flat cylinder whose teeth run parallel to its axis. The default
gear has 17 teeth at Module 1, a 20° pressure angle, and is 10 mm thick. The images show a 17-tooth gear
5 mm thick with a 3 mm bore and a 0.1 mm chamfer on its outer edges.

You can vary:

- Module, tooth count, pressure angle and thickness.
- A centered through-bore.
- A chamfer on the outer edge of both end faces.
- The position and orientation of the gear in space.

Dimensions are in millimeters, and the pressure angle is in radians. The tooth roots are filleted.

For how to build this gear from Go, see the [developer notes](../../docs/models/spurgear.md).
