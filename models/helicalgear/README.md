An involute gear whose teeth twist along its axis, so each tooth meets its mate gradually instead of all at
once. The twist, called the helix angle, runs from 1° to 45° across the gear's thickness. The images show a
17-tooth gear at Module 1 with a 3 mm bore and a 0.1 mm chamfer on its outer edges.

You can vary:

- Module, tooth count, pressure angle, thickness and helix angle.
- A centered through-bore.
- A chamfer on the outer edge of both end faces.
- The position and orientation of the gear in space.

A gear with no twist is a [spur gear](../spurgear/).

For how to build this gear from Go, see the [developer notes](../../docs/models/helicalgear.md).
